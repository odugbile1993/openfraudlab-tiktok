## In a nutshell

You have covered the core of data science, from data types to models and ethics. Next: practise Python, SQL and statistics regularly, do real projects, explain your results clearly, and share them in a portfolio.

## Key ideas

- **Look back:** You've covered a lot — Data, stats, models, ethics
- **Step 1:** Practise the basics — Python · SQL · statistics
- **Step 2:** Do real projects — Question → data → answer
- **Step 3:** Explain your work — Charts · plain words
- **Step 4:** Build a portfolio — Show, don't just tell
- **Takeaway:** Consistency over speed — Small steps, every week

## Try it yourself

Write a one-page plan: one question you want to answer with data, where you might find a dataset for it, and three steps you will take this week to start.

Data Science from Scratch, lesson thirty: your data science roadmap, and where to go from here.

Look how far you've come. You've learned data types, statistics, cleaning, charts, Python, SQL, models, evaluation, and ethics. That is the core of the field.

Step one: keep practising the foundations. Short, regular sessions with Python, pandas, SQL, and basic statistics usually beat occasional long ones.

Step two: do projects. Pick a real question, find a dataset, clean it, explore it, model it if needed, and write up what you found. Projects are where the ideas join together.

Step three: practise communicating. A good analysis that nobody understands has little impact. Explain your results in plain words, with one clear chart.

Step four: share your work. Put your projects on GitHub, or write short posts about them. A small portfolio often says more than a list of courses.

The real secret is consistency. Small steps, every week, add up faster than you might expect.
