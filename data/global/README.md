# Global practice datasets (lessons 10–30)

Synthetic datasets from several industries, generated by `scripts/make_global_data.py` with fixed random seeds. They behave like the real problems they represent but are not records of real people, companies or prices. Money is in US dollars.

| File | Field | Rows | What it is | Typical target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `customers.csv` | Retail / e-commerce | 2,000 | Online-store customers in 12 countries: signup date, country, age, segment, acquisition channel | — |
| `orders.csv` | Retail / e-commerce | 12,000 | Orders in 2024: date, customer, category, units, unit price, discount, shipping days, returned, rating (some missing) | `returned`, revenue |
| `bike_rentals.csv` | Transport / weather | 731 | Daily bike-share rentals for one city, 2023–2024, with temperature, humidity, wind, rain, holidays, weekends, and a nearby kiosk's ice-cream sales | `rentals` |
| `subscribers.csv` | Subscription / telecom-style churn | 4,000 | Streaming-service subscribers in six world regions: plan, fee, contract, tenure, usage, devices, support tickets, payment method, auto-renew | `churned` |
| `transactions.csv` | Payments / fraud | 20,000 | Card transactions: amount, hour, online, foreign, new merchant, distance from home, recent activity | `is_fraud` (about 0.9%) |
